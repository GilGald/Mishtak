Grofit Tzameret, Petah Tikva: apartments explorer
==================================================

How to open
-----------
1. Unzip the folder anywhere (keep all the files together).
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.
   No internet is needed: the apartment data, all plan PDFs and the 3D library are inside the folder.

What's inside
-------------
- Table tab: all 205 apartments with filters, sorting, prices, a mortgage calculator
  (your own money, years and interest give a monthly payment and mortgage per apartment),
  plan and floor-plan PDFs, and a 3D view per apartment type.
- "My turn": mark apartments as Taken as people ahead of you in the queue choose, and star (★)
  the ones you want. The ranked shortlist shows your best pick still available.
  The marks are saved in your own browser only. Use Export / Import to copy them between browsers or people.
- Room mix tab: pie charts of 3/4/5-room apartments per building.
- Sun & shadows (3D) tab: the three buildings with the sun for any date and time,
  plus a sun-hours heatmap per facade.

Notes
-----
- Data and plans were saved from the developer's price list (mishtaken.grofit.co.il) on 30 Sep 2026.
  Prices and availability may have changed since.
- Furniture in the 3D views is auto-placed for illustration. Building heights in the sun study are estimates.
- Mortgage figures are estimates (equal monthly payments) and exclude fees, taxes and indexation.
